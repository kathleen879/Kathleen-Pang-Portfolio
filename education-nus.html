What I learnt at the National University of Singapore

Bachelor of Science with Honours

Major in Data Science and Analytics

I am studying for a Bachelor of Science with Honours in Data Science and Analytics at the National University of Singapore, from 2023 to 2027.

I chose to study Data Science and Analytics because the Statistics that I learnt in Junior College intrigued me. Going into university, I walked into a new world of programming and code. Having no prior experience, the learning curve was steep and I struggled. I slowly started to see the beauty in having the ability to transform numbers in a spreadsheet to valuable insights that can guide decision making. In our world, data is king.

Relevant Modules: Linear Algebra, Advanced Calculus, Probability, Mathematical Statistics, Machine Learning, Convex Optimisation.
Skills learnt: R, Python, SQL, PowerBI, Power Automate, PowerApps, Microsoft Suite.

Global Education

In my time in NUS, I was also given the amazing opportunites to go for overseas experiences.

1. Semester Exchange in the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), Jan-June 2026

I spent 4 months living in Hong Kong. It was my first time living outside of Singapore for an extended period of time and with my grading on a pass/fail basis, I believe these 4 months was the most freedom I felt in my life. I truly got to immerse myself into the culture and gain exciting new experiences travelling around the region as well.



2. Summer Programme in Kyushu University, July 2026

These 2 weeks in Japan are 2 weeks that I will remember fondly for a long time. I stayed in the on campus dorm of Kyushu University, which was surrounded by the mountains and was nearby the sea. I loved it. I took modules learning about Robotics and the Environment from a Japanese perspective and also got to play the Taiko Japanese drums with the club members. During the orientation, I made a good friend, Asumi. I discovered we both enjoyed running and on my second last day in Japan, Asumi and I went on a lovely morning run and we had breakfast together. I got an onigiri and yoghurt from the 7-11 in the school canteen. When we parted ways, Asumi grabbed onto her bicycle and cycled home, which was one metro station away. I still vividly remember the scene of her cycling further and further away, until she disappeared into the mountains. It was amazing.
